Zero and Kanna support, a new character sidebar, and per-attempt Star Force planning

- Your roster now lives in a sidebar on the left, showing each character's HEXA score, stars and HEXA fragments, with totals at the bottom. A search box appears once you have six or more characters. Hide it with « for the full page width; the tab on the left edge brings it back.
- The character overview shows the Sol Erda Fragments spent on each character's HEXA skills. A "≥" means some HEXA levels are not scanned yet, or completed HEXA Stat cores are counted at their minimum cost. Sia Astelle and Erel Light are not totalled yet.

- Zero: Lazuli and Lapis are treated as one weapon. Star Force, flames and cubes are priced once, the secondary sword is marked as mirroring the weapon, and upgrade suggestions count the potential and flame Boss/Damage that both swords provide. Lazuli and Lapis Type 7-9 can now be planned with Star Force. The extra Astra secondary slot is not scanned yet.
- Kanna: Talisman stars are now read, including the 26-star limit, and priced with the correct weapon Star Force gains.
- Flame upgrade suggestions now work for weapons that roll normal flames, such as Zero's, by keeping their top Attack tier.

- Star Force: choose the enhancement mode for each attempt from 15★ to 21★ with sliders, or apply quick presets (111, 222, 333 or Safeguard; 1111, 1144 or 4444). Each attempt shows its success chance, destruction chance and cost with your event settings.
- Flames: choose Default, Scouter or Custom weights from the bar at the bottom of the Flames page, and enter your own weights to model other builds.
- Flames carried over by the old item transposition system, such as on some Sweetwater equipment, are recognized as legacy flames and are no longer priced as if they could be rolled again.

- Character Info scans now read the level and class on light or busy profile backgrounds. If a scan still fails there, the guide suggests switching to a plainer background.
- Scouter: Calculate is pinned to the bottom of the page, HEXA levels are capped at 30 as you type, buffs switch on and off by clicking their tile, and old calculations can be deleted from the Results tab.
- The potential and flame weight switches now sit in a bar at the bottom of the page, and combined costs are sorted lowest first, like the other tabs.
