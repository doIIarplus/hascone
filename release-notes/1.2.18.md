Sol Erda HEXA upgrade routes

- Choose Fragments or Sol Erda on the HEXA page to optimize for the resource limiting your upgrades. Both routes use your saved Scouter calculation and current HEXA levels.
- Save both routes separately for each calculation and remember your resource choice per character. Switching to a saved route reuses it without another Scouter request.
