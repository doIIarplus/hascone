Quanah: Dimensional missions, orbs and piece controls

- New Dimensional missions tab on the Quanah page. Check off each mission to see your grade, total and permanent points, with completed missions listed at the top. Completing a mission also completes the earlier ones in its chain (inspector levels, Battlesim floors, a boss's difficulties, star force), and undoing one undoes the later ones. Resetting the round missions asks you to confirm first.
- Pieces for another class now count toward your best set: their lines are orbed onto your own piece in that slot. When two sets tie, the one needing fewer orbs wins, and the best set shows how many orbs it needs.
- Mark a piece Preferred to lock it into its slot, Wearing to count an orb whenever another piece is used in that slot, or Disabled to leave it out of every set.
- When a complete set can be made in several ways, use the arrows to step through them, fewest orbs first. The combo you're viewing is highlighted in Your pieces.
- The GanDi build is now called Hybrid. Your saved choice carries over.
- Edit and Delete are now in each piece's menu.
- Fixed: hovering a piece in your inventory could read the equipped item instead, when the cursor covered the "Currently Equipped" label of the comparison tooltip.
