Fixes for the MapleStory skill window update and new characters

- Erda Link scans for Sia Astelle and Erel Light work again with the redesigned skill window. Open Skills → VI, scroll the list to the top for the first step and to the bottom for the second, and keep the cursor off the skill rows.
- Adding a character works again. Nexon moved its public rankings, which made every character lookup fail. Refreshing a character's image and level and looking up its world for Bossing are fixed too.
