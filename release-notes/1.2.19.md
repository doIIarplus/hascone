Target Star Force and interface polish

- Set a target star for any item and the upgrade order prices and ranks every star up to that target, not just the next one. Leave it blank to keep the old next-star behavior. Targets above an item's star limit are saved as that limit, and they survive rescans and item replacement.
- Neighboring steps for the same item appear as one combined row. Best per item still shows one row per item: its top upgrade plus the target steps that follow it.
- Compare upgrades again to refresh saved upgrade orders with the new Star Force ranking.
- Each upgrade list remembers its Show filter across tab switches and reloads.
- Whole-number fields with a maximum, such as Star Force target, Hyper Stat levels, flame values and Scouter tiles, snap to the maximum as you type.
- Tidier Star Force and gear panels: the custom modes table collapses into one card with an Edit/Hide toggle, current Star Force costs are merged into one section, the item scan button sits beside "Scan equipped gear…", and "Edit flame stats" appears only on the Flame tab.
