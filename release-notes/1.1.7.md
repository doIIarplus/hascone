More reliable character level scanning

- Fixed Character Info scans rejecting a clear level because OCR misread the tiny "Lv." prefix, producing errors such as "stat.level: Low confidence (91.31%; requires 97%)."
- When the full level label cannot be verified, the scanner now retries the level digits separately. Numeric confidence checks still apply, and conflicting readings are rejected.
- Verified the fix against the failing level-295 capture: the digits were read at 99.99% confidence, and the complete Character Info scan passed without errors.
