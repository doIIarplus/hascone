Fragment totals for Sia Astelle and Erel Light

- Sia Astelle and Erel Light now show the Sol Erda Fragments spent on their Erda Link skills on the overview cards, in the roster and in the account total. Scan Skills → VI in Scouter to fill them in.
- Their totals are a minimum (≥): Rush, Boost and SHINE Stones are not counted, because they do not appear as skill levels.
