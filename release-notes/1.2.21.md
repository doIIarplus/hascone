Quanah: event equipment set solver

- New Quanah page, pinned at the top of the roster. Pick your class, add your Libae's Supercharged hat, top, bottom, cape, gloves and shoes, and see the one-per-slot set that completes your 18 skill lines.
- Choose the Skill, GanDi or EU build, or set your own Custom build of up to 18 lines. The best set shows which lines you still need, and which single drop would finish it.
- Scan pieces instead of typing them: press Start scanning, then hover each piece in your inventory or Equipment. Every new tooltip is captured and read in the background, so you can keep hovering. Other items you pass over, such as shoulders or medals, are skipped.
- Pieces for another class are marked Can't wear and left out, so you don't spend resources on them. Identical pieces are marked Duplicate. Fix a misread line with the pencil, or delete a piece with ×.
- Supported scan settings are unchanged. Scanning at 2560 × 1440 has not been tested yet; if it doesn't pick up tooltips, add your pieces by hand.
